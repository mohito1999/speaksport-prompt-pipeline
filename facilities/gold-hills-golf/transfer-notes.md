# Transfer notes

Use only these exact Vapi destination identifiers. Do not include phone numbers in the prompt.

- `pro_shop`: tee-time assistance, modifications, rescheduling, cancellations within 24 hours, prepaid cancellation refunds, course conditions, merchandise, and general golf operations.
- `maintenance`: course maintenance, turf and grounds, agronomy, irrigation, and maintenance-team inquiries.
- `jeff_thomason`: requests for Golf Professional Jeff Thomason by name or title, golf instruction, and professional golf-service inquiries.
- `grill_restaurant`: Grill/Restaurant hours, dining, menus, food and beverage service, reservations, and restaurant inquiries.
- `chad_white`: requests for Director of Golf Chad White by name or title, golf-department leadership, and escalated golf-operation inquiries.
- `beth_havsgaard`: requests for Office Manager Beth Havsgaard by name or title, office administration, billing, paperwork, and administrative inquiries.

Before every transfer, check Current Operating Status. If it is `after_hours`,
never call the transfer tool; explain that the team is closed, offer to help,
and provide Opening Time as the callback time.

During open hours, a caller's explicit direct request is already consent and
must not be reconfirmed. Gold Hills enables one softer first-request Pro Shop
assistance check: "Is there something I can assist you with first?" If the
caller declines or still wants the transfer, execute it without another
confirmation question. When the assistant offers a transfer on its own, ask
once, stop, and transfer after the caller accepts.
