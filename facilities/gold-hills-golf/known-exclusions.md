# Known exclusions

- Do not copy Sugarmill Woods or Citrus National facts, rules, staff, destinations, or course names.
- Do not include phone numbers in the prompt; Vapi transfer destinations own them.
- Do not mention backend endpoint versions.
- Do not claim that the Pro Shop is busy.
- Do not ask callers to reconfirm a direct transfer request.
- Do not call the transfer tool when `{{current_status}}` is `after_hours`.
- Do not pass `course` or `course_name` to availability.
- Do not ask riding or walking before availability.
- Do not quote fee-inclusive `price_per_player` fields or describe a SpeakSport booking fee; Gold Hills uses returned base walking and riding rates.
- Do not offer a single player a slot with all four spots remaining.
