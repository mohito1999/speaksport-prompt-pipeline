# Transfer notes

Use only these exact Vapi destination identifiers. Do not include phone numbers in the prompt.

- `pro_shop`: tee-time assistance, booking changes, cancellations within 24 hours, course conditions, and general golf operations.
- `events`: golf outings, tournaments, private events, and event planning.
- `bar`: bar hours, dining, beverage service, and lounge inquiries.
- `kelli`: Kelli inquiries and administration.
- `mark_gm`: General Manager Mark inquiries and executive facility management.

Normal integrated transfers follow the two-step confirmation protocol from the integrated reference prompt.
