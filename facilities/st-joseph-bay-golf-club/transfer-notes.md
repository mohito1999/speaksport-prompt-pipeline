Use only these exact Vapi destination identifiers. Do not include phone numbers in the prompt.

- `golf_shop`: tee-time assistance, booking changes, cancellations, course conditions, and general golf operations.
- `golf_instruction`: lessons, clinics, coaching, and instructor inquiries.
- `pool`: questions about the St. Joseph Bay Golf Club Pool.
- `events`: outings, tournaments, private events, and event planning.
- `pomodoro_italian_grill_bar`: restaurant hours, dining questions, reservations, and Pomodoro Italian Grill & Bar inquiries.

Normal integrated transfers follow the two-step confirmation protocol from the integrated reference prompt.
