# Approved official wedding-site facts

Source: https://www.weddingsattwinoaks.com/

- Weddings at Twin Oaks Golf Course is a San Diego-area wedding and special
  event venue at Twin Oaks.
- The venue offers ceremony and reception packages, including an all-inclusive
  option and mix-and-match planning.
- The venue offers private venue tours.
- Event types include weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, baby and bridal
  showers, graduations, first birthdays, corporate gatherings, and holiday
  events.
- The property offers indoor and outdoor event spaces, ample parking, and
  customizable packages and menus.
- Named spaces include the Garden Ballroom, Garden Vista Lawn, Fireside Patio,
  Bridal Suite and Grooms Room, and The Bevy.
- The venue describes gathering sizes from 20 to 200 guests. The JC Golf Twin
  Oaks page separately states wedding and event capacity up to 225 guests; do
  not silently choose between these figures when exact capacity matters.
- Current published availability says final 2026 wedding dates remain and
  weddings are being booked throughout 2027. Treat this as time-sensitive.
- Wedding-site questions about exact packages, prices, availability, catering,
  bar service, capacities, or planning should be routed to the Weddings at Twin
  Oaks Team when the approved knowledge base does not provide the answer.
