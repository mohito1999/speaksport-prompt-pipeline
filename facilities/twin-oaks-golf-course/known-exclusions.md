# Known exclusions

- Exclude every other JC Golf course. Shared navigation, cross-promotions, and
  general JC Golf pages must not cause another facility's facts to enter the
  Twin Oaks knowledge base.
- Do not include phone numbers in the prompt; Vapi owns transfer routing.
- Do not invent a nine-hole booking flow. Always use 18 holes.
- Do not ask riding or walking. Twin Oaks rates include a cart and booking uses
  `riding: true`.
- Do not grant the eight-day window to JC 20/30 Club or JC Junior Player Card
  holders.
- Do not treat SoCal or Senior discounts as eligibility rules.
- Do not add a `TTID_` prefix to Club Prophet numeric booking references.
- Do not state that cancellation automatically refunds a prepaid booking.
- Do not copy facility facts or destinations from the reference prompt.
- Do not mention backend endpoint versions.
