- Do not copy Bobby Jones Golf Course, Murray Golf House, or other reference
  facility facts, policies, departments, staff, destinations, or course names.
- Do not include phone numbers in the prompt; Vapi transfer destinations own
  them.
- Do not claim the assistant can directly search, hold, book, modify, reschedule,
  look up, or cancel tee times.
- Do not enable or mention integrated eligibility, inventory, booking, lookup,
  or cancellation tools.
- Do not invent responsibilities or job titles for named transfer destinations.
- Do not mention backend endpoint versions.
