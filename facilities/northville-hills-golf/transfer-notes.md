# Transfer notes

Use only these exact Vapi destination identifiers. Do not include phone numbers in the prompt.

- `pro_shop`: tee-time assistance, booking changes, cancellations, course conditions, and general golf operations.
- `banquet_sales`: banquets, weddings, corporate events, and private function sales inquiries.
- `golf_pro`: Head Golf Professional inquiries, instruction, and golf pro services.
- `general_manager`: General Manager (GM) management and executive facility inquiries.
- `banquet_bar`: banquet bar hours, lounge, and event bar inquiries.

Normal integrated transfers follow the two-step confirmation protocol from the integrated reference prompt.
