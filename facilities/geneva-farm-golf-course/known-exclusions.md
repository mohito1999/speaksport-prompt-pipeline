# Known exclusions

- Do not copy facts, policies, staff, destinations, or course names from any
  reference facility.
- Do not include phone numbers in the prompt; Vapi transfer destinations own
  them.
- Do not apply the ForeUp `TTID_` booking-reference convention. Geneva Farm
  uses numeric Club Prophet references such as `366135`.
- Do not invent booking restrictions beyond the supplied 10-calendar-day
  inclusive window and past-date guard.
- Do not state that cancelling a prepaid tee time automatically issues a refund.
- Do not mention backend endpoint versions.
